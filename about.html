About

I build systems, not just posts

My name is Philip Oladele. I am a certified professional social media strategist. I make things happen, and I am good at what I do.

I am not here to just manage your social media, post content, or chase likes. I am here to understand your brand, see where it can go, and help make that vision a reality. This is not just something I do. It is my calling.

Over the years I have worked with authors and brands including Michael Vecchione, Jimmy Dennedy, Benjamin Moe, Teresa Michael, B.G. Howard, Aryn Alyssabeth, Meagan Poetschlag, R.L. Corbit, Jessica King, and Victoria Reiby.

Your success as a business or brand is directly proportional to my success as your partner. I take that very seriously.

Measurement

Social media attribution models

We do not guess which post created a client. We map the path from first touch to paid work, then pick the model that matches the campaign.

01

First touch

Credit goes to the first post, ad or search that introduced the brand. Used for awareness and new author launches.

02

Last touch

Credit goes to the final action before the message, sale or booking. Used for landing pages and launch-week CTAs.

03

Linear

Every step in the path shares credit. Used when content, community and ads all work together over weeks.

04

Time decay

Later touches get more credit. Used when a trailer or event post closes people who already know the work.

05

Position based

First and last touches take most of the credit. The middle content still counts. Good for book funnels.

06

UTM and platform mix

Links, landing pages and inbox notes carry tags so Facebook, Instagram, email and ads can be compared without vanity metrics.

Growth

Launch

Rights

Offer

Unique codes and tagged links so we can see which creator moved sales, not only likes.

Fit

Audience match

We pick people whose readers already buy books, services or local offers. Reach without fit is wasted spend.
